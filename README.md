# hr-attrition-predictor
A dataset analyzer that predicts causes of employee attrition using IBM HR dataset

Testing hypothesis one - I believe that relationship with manager at higher ratings will lead to less attrition due to a friendlier environment making the employee want to retain their position

Testing hypothesis two - I believe that the higher the years at company within the IT department will lead to more attrition as the IT job market tends to pay higher across job moves rather than promotions
